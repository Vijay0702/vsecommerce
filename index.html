About VS Ecommerce

VS Ecommerce is a small, family-run online retail business based in Peterborough, owned by Vijay Bhashamalla. We buy stock directly from brands, manufacturers and established UK wholesalers, and sell it to customers across the UK on Amazon.

Our stock is stored and shipped by Amazon's fulfilment network, so customers get fast delivery and Amazon's own customer service, and suppliers get a partner who keeps their products in stock and well presented.

We only sell genuine, new products bought from legitimate sources, and we keep full invoices for everything we list.

Supplier questions

Do you sell anywhere other than Amazon?

Amazon UK is our main sales channel. If you have rules about where your products can be sold, tell us and we'll follow them.

Can you provide trade references and business details?

Yes. Contact us and we'll send our full business details for your trade account application.

Will you undercut my other retailers?

No. We follow suppliers' pricing policies and aim to keep a healthy, stable price on Amazon.

Customer questions

Are your products genuine?

Yes. Every product is new and bought from the brand or an authorised supplier, with invoices.

Who delivers my order?

Our orders are packed and shipped by Amazon, so delivery options and tracking are shown in your Amazon account.

How do I return something?

Start a return from Your Orders in your Amazon account. Returns follow Amazon's returns policy.
